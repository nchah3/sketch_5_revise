Animation details & outside sources

The ghost floats and gently sways. The pumpkin grows and shrinks as its face changes from orange to yellow. The bat follows an elliptical path, tilts, and flaps its wings. A slowly changing purple sky, fading stars, and a glowing moon connect everything to the Halloween theme.

Bonus features: changing background colors, random star positions and sizes, arrays, synchronized pumpkin effects, and push() / pop() for transformations.

AI assistance: NetNet AI helped with the coding problems that was happening inside the code, while Gemini provided the official p5js sources on how to get the movement of my three shapes to move smoothly.

References and how they apply to this sketch: Class tutorial, Introto_p5js.pdf, for setup(), draw(), shapes, variables, loops, arrays, colors, and media loading. Additional official p5.js references are grouped below. The matching [R1]-[R8] comments explain their use in the code.

  • [R1] Animation timing: frameCount supplies a changing frame number. Multiplying it by 0.02 controls how quickly the animation phase advances.
  • [R2] Repeating movement: sin() and cos() control the ghost's motion, the bat's elliptical path, and wing flapping.
  • [R3] Changing number ranges: map() converts the repeating values into useful sizes and opacity values.
  • [R4] Color and transparency: lerpColor() blends the sky and pumpkin-face colors. The forth argument of fill() controls opacity, creating the fading stars and translucent glow.
  • [R5] Moving and rotating groups: translate() places each character's drawing origin; rotate() tilts it. push() and pop() keep those changes inside that character's group.
  • [R6] Random stars: random() selects star positions and sizes once in setup(); arrays store them.
  • [R7] Image loading: The p5.js 1.x preload() reference and loadImage() reference explain loading the moon before setup(). This sketch uses p5.js 1.11.3.
  • [R8] Keyboard controls: noLoop() and loop() pauses and resumes drawing; saveCanvas() exports a PNG when S is pressed.
